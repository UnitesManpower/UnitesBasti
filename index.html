About UNITES

Building skills. Assessing talent. Connecting opportunities.

UNITES Testing Training Centre, Basti is a branch of UNITES Manpower Consultant. The registered office is in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, and the Basti branch supports trade testing, training, candidate assessment and recruitment-related enquiries.

  • Candidate and employer enquiry support
  • Job requirement communication
  • Interview and application guidance
  • Local office support in Basti

We encourage every candidate to understand the job role, employer details, salary, benefits, contract terms and applicable process before making a decision. Contact our team to confirm the latest information for a specific vacancy.
